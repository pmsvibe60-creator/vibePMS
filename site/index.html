THE USEFUL DETAILS

Before you fork.

Is this a finished property management app?+

It’s a starter kit: Twenty CRM provides the operator interface and records, while the TypeScript sidecar adds rental workflows. You’ll need to configure services, test your use case and make it your own.

What’s free, and what needs an account?+

The original vibePMS code is MIT licensed. Hosting, email and payment services are separate and may cost money. Twenty and other dependencies retain their own licenses. The CasaPay integration requires a separate account.

Can I use it outside Estonia?+

You can adapt the source, but the current templates and rental rules are oriented toward Estonia. Localize and review the legal, tax, payment and signing requirements for your market.

Can I help build it?+

Yes. Browse the open issues, read the contribution guide, or start a conversation in Discussions.
